## Slide 2

What AI Can Do For Us --- Part 1

✦ BENEFITS & OPPORTUNITIES

Medicine & Health

- AI accelerates drug discovery --- new treatments in months, not decades
- Diagnosis from medical imaging often rivals or exceeds specialist accuracy
- Personalized treatment plans based on genomics and patient history
- 24/7 mental health support and cognitive behavioral therapy chatbots
- Pandemic modeling and outbreak early-warning systems

Science & Research

- AlphaFold solved the 50-year protein-folding problem
- AI-guided materials discovery for batteries, semiconductors, and catalysts
- Accelerating climate modeling and extreme-weather prediction
- Automated analysis of telescope, genomics, and particle-physics data
- Writing research code and literature reviews in minutes

## Slide 3

What AI Can Do For Us --- Part 2

✦ BENEFITS & OPPORTUNITIES

Education

- Personalized tutoring adapts to each student\'s pace and gaps
- Instant feedback on writing, math, and coding assignments
- High-quality education accessible in any language, anywhere
- Students with dyslexia or ADHD gain new accommodations
- Lifelong learning made frictionless for adults re-skilling

Productivity & Economy

- Coding assistants dramatically raise developer output
- Automated summarization, translation, and drafting saves hours daily
- Small businesses gain capabilities previously reserved for large firms
- Legal and financial research democratized for ordinary people
- New industries and job categories not yet imagined

## Slide 4

What AI Can Do For Us --- Part 3

✦ BENEFITS & OPPORTUNITIES

Safety & Infrastructure

- Autonomous vehicles could eliminate \~90% of human-error accidents
- AI detects cyberattacks and financial fraud faster than any human team
- Structural monitoring predicts bridge and building failures before they happen
- Wildfire and flood prediction gives earlier, better warnings
- Air traffic optimization reduces fuel burn and delays

Equity & Access

- Real-time translation breaks language barriers in healthcare and law
- Vision and hearing assistive tools restore independence
- Free, expert-quality advice on legal rights and benefits for low-income users
- Rare-disease diagnosis assistance for patients in underserved regions
- Creative tools empower artists without expensive equipment or training

Discussion Questions

- Which risks are already occurring, and which remain speculative?
